Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q1: How can I book a cab with Lankadhish Cab Service?

You can easily book a cab by calling us directly or messaging us via WhatsApp.

Q2: Are cab services available at night?

Yes, our cab service operates 24/7 (around the clock).

Q3: What types of cars are available in your fleet?

We provide a wide range of clean and comfortable vehicles, including Hatchbacks, Sedans, SUVs, and luxury cars for weddings or corporate travel.

Q4: Are toll and parking charges included in the fare?

Generally, toll tax, state permits, and parking charges are additional and payable by the customer based on actual costs, unless explicitly specified in a fixed package.

Q5: Can I book a cab for a round trip outside the city?

Yes, we specialize in both one-way drops and outstation round trips with experienced drivers.
